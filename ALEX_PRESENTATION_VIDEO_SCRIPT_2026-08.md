## Statements deliberately removed

The replacement does not claim a prebuilt connection to 500 systems, a free trial, direct subscription pricing, a guaranteed implementation time, guaranteed ROI, or a guaranteed SLA. It correctly states that integrations are selected and delivered per customer pilot after technical and security assessment.

## Language and visual direction

The spoken content is English, matching the current public presentation video. The visual direction should retain ALEX’s professional, calm, confident on-camera presence, the dark navy IntelligentForce background, and cyan accents.
